Who We Are?

Know About Us

Afghanistan Snowboard began its journey in 2017 by officially joining the World Snowboard Federation (WSF). The organization was founded with the belief that winter sports, particularly snowboarding, can be a force for positive change. What started as a small initiative, using modified skateboards in the mountains, quickly grew into a national movement, connecting young people from diverse backgrounds through the thrill of snowboarding.

From the very beginning, our goal was not only to build athletes but to build a better society. We saw snowboarding as a dynamic and exciting way to engage Afghan youth, encourage peace, and promote social inclusion. Our programs have brought joy and purpose to countless young people, offering them a sense of identity, confidence, and global connection.

Snowboarding, for us, is more than a sport. It is a message of hope, resilience, and peace.

Our Journey

Key Milestones

National competitions, youth training camps and international events. In 2025 we made history by representing Afghanistan at the Winter Asian Games in Harbin, China.

  1. 2017Founded

    Afghanistan Snowboard officially joins the World Snowboard Federation.

  2. 2018January

    First election of the governing body, beginning official activities as one team.

  3. 2019September

    The first sandboarding event in Afghanistan takes boards to the dunes.

  4. 2020Nov – Dec

    First methodical snowboarding seminar in Kabul and the first youth program in Panjshir.

  5. 2025February

    Afghanistan competes at the Asian Winter Games in Harbin, China.

Mission & Vision

Our Mission

Afghanistan Snowboard is an independent, nonprofit, and government-recognized organization dedicated to advancing snowboarding across Afghanistan. Since our founding in 2017, we have worked to promote the sport as a platform for youth empowerment, social inclusion, and national pride. We aim to inspire the next generation of Afghan athletes while fostering a sense of peace, unity, and opportunity through winter sports.

Our Vision

Our vision is to establish Afghanistan as a thriving hub for snowboarding in the region and beyond. We believe in using sport to reflect the strength, resilience, and beauty of Afghan society. Through meaningful programs and international partnerships, we aspire to represent a peaceful and hopeful image of Afghanistan to the world, one built on youth leadership, gender equality, and positive community transformation.

What We Work Toward

Our Goals

Eight commitments guide every program, camp and competition we run, from the slopes of Kabul to the international stage.

Join Us

  • To show a new and peaceful face of Afghanistan to the world through the sport of snowboarding.

  • To create equal opportunities for athletes, regardless of gender or background, to compete and grow.

  • To promote women's participation in snowboarding, supporting their voices and visibility in society.

  • To develop national and international-level training programs, competitions, and camps.

  • To build strong relationships between Afghan athletes and global peers through collaboration and cultural exchange.

  • To advocate for youth involvement in healthy lifestyles and help prevent drug use and social isolation.

  • To acquire advanced training equipment and facilities to support athlete development.

  • To be a unifying voice for peace, resilience, and national pride through sport.

What We Stand For

Core Values

Peace through Sport

We use snowboarding as a tool to unite communities, foster harmony, and promote non-violence.

Inclusivity

Our programs are open to individuals of all genders, ethnicities, and social backgrounds.

Empowerment

Proud Moments

Our Achievements

  • Official membership in the World Snowboard Federation and recognition by the International Ski and Snowboard Federation (FIS).

  • Historic representation of Afghanistan in the Winter Asian Games (2025).
