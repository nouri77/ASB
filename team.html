Founder & President

Ahmad Romal Hayat

A lifelong athlete and advocate for youth empowerment, Romal started Afghanistan Snowboard in 2016 with a bold vision: to introduce snowboarding to Afghan youth as a way to inspire peace, build resilience, and create opportunity in a conflict-affected nation.

What began as a grassroots effort with modified skateboards grew into a national movement. Under his leadership the organization joined the WSF and FIS, organized national competitions, launched youth training camps, and in 2025 represented Afghanistan at the Winter Asian Games for the first time.

Even in adversity, dreams can take flight on snow-covered peaks.
