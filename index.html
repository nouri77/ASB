Who We Are

Empowering Youth Through Winter Sports

Founded in 2017, the Afghanistan Snowboard Organization (ASO) is a government-recognized nonprofit dedicated to promoting snowboarding across Afghanistan. We believe in the power of sport to drive youth empowerment, foster peace, and promote social inclusion.

Despite challenges, our athletes represent Afghanistan on the international stage, proving that resilience and passion can overcome any obstacle. We are committed to gender equality and providing opportunities for all youth to experience the joy of snowboarding.

"Sport as a mirror of Afghan strength, resilience, and beauty."

Our Mission

Promote snowboarding as a platform for youth empowerment, social inclusion, and national pride. We provide equipment, training, and a safe environment for all to learn and grow.

Our Vision

Establish Afghanistan as a thriving regional snowboarding hub; represent peace and hope through sport on the global stage, producing competitive athletes for international events.
